# Price-data research

Use price histories, source timestamps, currency conversions and calculation
methods to understand differences between card markets. Estimates should expose
their inputs and uncertainty. A historical price is not an executable offer.

The former API sales tiers, acquisition quotas, MRR targets and launch schedule
are retired. This work has no commercial milestone or requirement to fund itself.
Access controls, source permissions, rate limits and infrastructure cost controls
remain in place. Existing agreements, payouts and records retain their terms.

The [January 2026 analysis](history/API_COMMERCIALIZATION_STRATEGY-before-2026-09-24.md.txt)
is preserved as history, including its schema sketches and market observations.
Validate those dated details against current source before relying on them; the
archived commercial plan is not an instruction to launch a paid API.
